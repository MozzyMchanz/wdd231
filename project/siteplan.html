Site Plan: Nexoth Physiotherapy & Wellness Portal

WDD 231 Web Frontend Development Individual Project

1. Site Name

Site Name: Nexoth Physiotherapy & Wellness Portal

Reasoning: The name combines "nexus" (a central connection point) and "growth," reflecting the clinic's mission to connect patients with personalized healing and long-term physical wellness. It offers a modern, authoritative identity that builds trust with prospective patients.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Nexoth Physiotherapy & Wellness Portal is to serve as an accessible digital hub for prospective and existing patients. The site communicates specialized care programs (such as Sports Injury Rehab, Post-Surgical Recovery, and Pain Management), facilitates online appointment inquiries, and delivers educational resources including downloadable exercise guides and video demonstrations to support movement health.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1 (New Patient with Acute Injury): "I recently injured my knee while running. Does Nexoth offer specialized sports rehabilitation, how do I submit an intake request, and how quickly can I be seen?"
  • Scenario 2 (Existing Patient seeking Home Exercises): "My therapist assigned specific lumbar stretches during my last visit. Where can I view clear video demonstrations or download printable PDF guides to ensure I perform them safely at home?"

4. Color Schema

The color palette creates a calm, professional, and accessible healthcare environment:

Primary (#1E3A8A)
Headers & Primary CTAs
Secondary (#0D9488)
Accents, Borders & Hovers
Background (#F8FAFC)
Body Container Area
Text (#1F2937)
Paragraph Content

5. Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat

Applied to page titles (<h1>), subheadings (<h2>, <h3>), and main action buttons to convey structure and modern professionalism.

Body Font: Open Sans

Applied to paragraph body copy (<p>), list items (<li>), form fields, and modal text due to its outstanding legibility across all viewport sizes.
